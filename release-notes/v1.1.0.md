## What's new in Billforce 1.1

Everything new is **optional**. It stays switched off, with no extra screens, until you turn it on in
**Settings & data → Business settings**. Your existing bills, customers and accounts keep working as before.

### GST (Business settings → GST)
- Choose **Not registered** (works exactly as before), **Registered (regular)** or **Composition scheme**.
- Registered: GST rate and HSN code on items, tax invoices with CGST/SGST or IGST, GST on returns and purchases
  (input tax credit), and **Pay GST** to set off credit and record the payment.
- Reports for filing: GST summary (GSTR-3B), sales register with B2B / B2C (GSTR-1), HSN summary, purchase
  register and composition summary (CMP-08). All can be saved as Excel.
- Choose once whether your prices include GST.

### Stock (Business settings → Stock & menu)
- Turn on stock tracking to see how much of each item you have. Bills take goods out; purchases and returns bring
  them in.
- Low-stock alerts on the dashboard, stock counts and adjustments (damaged, expired, own use), opening stock and
  the full stock history of every item.
- Stock is valued at average purchase cost in Profit & Loss and the Balance Sheet. Cashiers see quantities, not
  cost prices.
- You can still sell when stock shows zero; Billforce warns you.

### Restaurant menu (Business settings → Stock & menu)
- Add dishes, for example Butter Chicken, with a recipe listing the exact ingredients and quantities (g, kg, ml,
  litre, pieces).
- With stock tracking on, selling a dish takes its ingredients out of stock and warns you when one is running
  out.
- Ingredients are bought in purchase bills but never shown on the billing screen.
- The Menu costing report shows what each dish costs to make and its food cost %.

### New look
- A fresh, calm teal design with larger text, rounded cards and a light sidebar that is easy on the eyes all day.

### Updating
- Install over your current version. Your data stays where it is.
- The first time 1.1 opens your data, it keeps a copy of the old file next to it
  (`billforce-before-update-v….db` in `%APPDATA%\Billforce`), just in case.
- As always, take a backup to a pen drive now and then (Settings & data → Backup & restore).

### Downloads
- **Billforce-Setup-1.1.0.exe**: installer (recommended). No administrator password is needed.
- **Billforce-Portable-1.1.0.exe**: runs without installing.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" for new apps: click *More info → Run anyway*.
